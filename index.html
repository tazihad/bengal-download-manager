Engineering Excellence

Built for Performance and Precision

Bengal Download Manager blends the power of Aria2 daemon multi-chunking with the smooth responsiveness of modern Qt6 and KDE Kirigami.

⚡

Multi-Segment Acceleration

Downloads files in parallel chunks simultaneously to bypass server throttling and maximize your internet connection speed.

🌐

One-Click Browser Catching

Native browser extension intercepts download links automatically and transfers them to BDM over a secure local TCP loopback port (56800).

⏯️

Resilient Pause & Auto-Resume

Never start over again. Stop downloads at will or let the auto-reconnect engine resume automatically after Wi-Fi or network drops.

🎬

Integrated Media Downloader

Paste video/audio URLs to fetch stream metadata, preview thumbnails, and select resolutions and bitrates effortlessly.

🗂️

Smart Categorization

Auto-sorts incoming files into organized folders: Documents, Videos, Music, Compressed, and Programs based on mime-types.

🎨

Adaptive KDE Breeze Theming

Seamlessly inherits system light and dark themes with configurable accent colors and typography featuring OpenType tabular figures.
