Zero Data Collection

Privacy Policy

Bengal Download Manager and Bengal DM Connector do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or telemetry. Everything stays on your device.

No Tracking • No Telemetry • No Analytics

1. We Do Not Collect Any Data

Bengal Download Manager (the desktop application) and Bengal DM Connector (the browser extension) are designed to operate entirely on your local device. Neither the application nor the extension collects, stores, transmits, or shares any personal information, download history, browsing data, file metadata, IP addresses, or device identifiers.

2. What Bengal Download Manager Does Locally

Bengal Download Manager is a native desktop download manager powered by the Aria2 engine. All download operations, transfer queues, file paths, and settings are stored locally on your machine. The application communicates with your browser extension over a local loopback connection only — it does not phone home to any remote server.

  • Download lists, history, and settings are saved locally on your device only.
  • Files are downloaded directly from the source URL to your local storage.
  • No download metadata, URLs, or file contents are uploaded or shared with any third party.

3. Bengal DM Connector (Browser Extension)

The Bengal DM Connector browser extension (Chrome / Manifest V3 and Firefox) intercepts download triggers in your browser and forwards them to Bengal Download Manager over a secure local TCP loopback port (56800). The extension does not collect browsing history, page contents, or any personal data. All communication is confined to your local machine.

Local-only communication: The desktop app and browser extension talk to each other exclusively over localhost. No data leaves your computer, and no external server is involved.

4. No Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any analytics providers, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or telemetry platforms. There are no tracking pixels, cookies, or beacons associated with the application or extension.

5. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under any age.

6. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no data to leak or expose. All data that the application handles — your downloads — remains under your control on your own device.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of Bengal Download Manager or Bengal DM Connector after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project repository or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
